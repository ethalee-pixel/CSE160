Treasure Shift

Explore three different treasure zones, work through harder maze-style routes, collect every relic, and use special vases to survive the more dramatic lighting and portal effects.

Click start, use the mouse to look around, and collect every glowing relic you can find.

Controls: W A S D move, mouse look, Space jump. Jump boost lets you climb onto wall tops, and the portal unlocks after you collect all treasure in a level.

Feature Toggles

Grader Checklist

  • Basic scene: the world uses more than 3 primary shapes, including cubes, spheres, cylinders, cones, and torus forms, with animated treasure, portal, tower ring, and flame objects.
  • Directional light + perspective camera: a directional sun light is active in the scene, and the view uses a Three.js PerspectiveCamera.
  • Textured primary shapes: textured grass ground, wall blocks, and tree foliage/trunks are visible in the world.
  • Custom textured 3D model: the outpost tent is loaded from OBJ + MTL files.
  • Camera controls: pointer lock mouse look plus W A S D movement and Space to jump.
  • At least 3 light sources: ambient, hemisphere, directional, point, and spot lights are all included, and the toggle panel lets you verify them individually.
  • Skybox: a six-face skybox surrounds the scene.
  • At least 20 primary shapes: there are well over 20 visible meshes across the three playable levels.
  • Maze gameplay update: each level now uses denser wall layouts so the treasures are deeper in side paths and more difficult to find.

Extra Features

  • Playable 3-level treasure hunt structure with portal progression and replay support.
  • Four vase effects: speed boost, blackout trap, jump boost, and oracle reveal glow.
  • Teleport-to-start rescue button so players can recover if they get stuck in the maze.
  • Animated lighting controls with visible point-light cubes so the grader can verify the light setup directly.

WOW Features

  • Harder maze-style layouts that make relic hunting feel more like exploration instead of open wandering.
  • Jump boost now lets the player land on and traverse wall tops, opening alternate routes through the maze.
  • Animated crystal beacons with floating cores, rotating rings, and pulsing light columns.
  • Moving firefly swarms and atmospheric fog to make the world feel more alive.
  • The scene combines lighting, animated props, maze navigation, and vertical traversal into a more dramatic adventure-space instead of a simple object showcase.
